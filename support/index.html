FastKeyboard`

Welcome to the support page for FastKeyboard`. Tap letters as usual. Flick up for the symbol shown above the key.

How it works

Digits sit on the top row, brackets and quotes in the middle, and the marks that start or end a phrase on the bottom row. Flick up on a letter to insert that symbol. Shift does not change it.

Enable the keyboard

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard…
  3. Choose FastKeyboard from the list.
  4. While typing, long-press the globe or the emoji key and switch to FastKeyboard.

Privacy

FastKeyboard` does not request Allow Full Access. It has no network access and does not collect what you type. Details are in the privacy policy.
