Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version

FastKeyboard` is a custom iOS keyboard. You tap a letter, or flick up for the symbol above it, and iOS inserts that character into the app you are typing in.

We do not collect, store, or transmit anything you type. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no server.

1. What happens to what you type

Each character is handed to the current text field through Apple’s text input system. FastKeyboard` does not copy that text, keep a history of it, or send it off your device.

The keyboard does not read the rest of the document. The only thing it reads from the text system is the return-key label — Search, Go, Return, and so on — so that key can show the right word.

2. Full Access

FastKeyboard` does not request Allow Full Access. With Full Access off, iOS does not let a custom keyboard use the network or share storage with its companion app.

This app has no network code and no shared container. Typing stays on the device, in the app you are typing in.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Anything you type, including passwords and messages
  • Name, email address, phone number, or other account data
  • Contacts, photos, location, or device identifiers
  • Usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or a profile of you

The app does not require an account or login, and it has no in-app purchases.

4. Data stored on your device

FastKeyboard` does not write settings, history, or typing data to your device. Layout and gesture behavior are compiled into the app.

Deleting FastKeyboard` removes it. There is no copy of your data on our side, because none was collected.

5. Third-party services

The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting kits, and it does not contact any server we operate.

6. Apple

If you share diagnostics with Apple, Apple may collect installation counts, crash reports, and performance metrics under Apple’s Privacy Policy. That information is processed by Apple. We do not add our own analytics on top of it.

7. Children’s privacy

FastKeyboard` is a general-audience utility. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. GDPR, CCPA, and CalOPPA

We do not collect personal information, so there is no profile to access, correct, or delete, and nothing to sell or share. You can stop using the keyboard, or delete the app, at any time.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, the date at the top of the page will change.
